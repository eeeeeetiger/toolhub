JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF — four letters that decide file size, quality, and whether a browser even shows your image. Here's the quick version, plus how to switch between them privately.

## The formats at a glance

| Format | Best for | Transparency | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| JPG | Photos | No | Medium |
| PNG | Logos, screenshots, anything with transparency | Yes | Large |
| WebP | Web images (modern sites) | Yes | Small |
| AVIF | Web images where size matters most | Yes | Smallest |

**Rule of thumb:** photos → JPG or WebP; anything needing a see-through background → PNG or WebP; maximum web speed → AVIF.
