PDFs and images go hand in hand: you scan paper into a PDF, or you need a PDF page as a picture for a slide. Here's how to move in both directions — locally.

## PDF → images

When you need one page as a JPG or PNG (for a thumbnail, a print, or to edit in an image tool), use our [PDF to image tool](/tools/pdf-to-image):

1. Drop the PDF in.
2. Pick the pages and the output format (JPG/PNG).
3. Download the images.

This is handy for pulling a chart out of a report or a signature out of a contract.

## Images → PDF

When you have photos or scanned pages that belong in one document, use our [image to PDF tool](/tools/image-to-pdf):

1. Add your images in order.
2. Adjust page size if needed.
3. Download the PDF.

## Keep it private

Both tools run in your browser. **The PDF and images never leave your device** — important for anything containing personal data, contracts, or IDs.

## Build a local flow

- Scan → [image to PDF](/tools/image-to-pdf) → [compress](/tools/pdf-compress) → [merge](/tools/pdf-merge) with other docs.
- PDF → [PDF to image](/tools/pdf-to-image) → [remove background](/tools/ai-photo-cutout) on a page → reassemble.

Because every step is on-device, you can chain them without ever uploading a sensitive file. Compare that to cloud services that take a copy at each hop.
