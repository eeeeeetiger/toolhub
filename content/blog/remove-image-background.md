A clean, background-free image makes products look professional and profile pictures look sharp. But background removers are exactly the kind of tool people hesitate to trust with a photo — and rightly so, since many upload your image to a remote server.

## What you can do with a transparent background

- Put a product on any colored store page.
- Make a polished LinkedIn or avatar photo.
- Drop a cutout into a flyer or slideshow.

## Remove the background locally

Our [AI background remover](/tools/ai-photo-cutout) runs the model **in your browser**:

1. Upload your image (it stays on your device).
2. The AI detects the subject and cuts out the background.
3. Refine edges with the brush if needed, then download a PNG with transparency.

Because the model runs on your machine, **the photo is never sent to a server**. This is ideal for product shots, ID photos, or anything you'd rather not share.

## Clean up first

- A plain, contrasting background gives the best auto-cut.
- For batch work (many product photos), our [batch background remover](/tools/batch-background-remover) handles several at once, still locally.
- Cut the subject, then [compress the PNG](/tools/image-compressor) before uploading it to your store.
