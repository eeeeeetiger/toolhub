iPhone voice memos, WhatsApp audio, and plenty of recorders save as **M4A**. It's a fine format, but older players, car stereos, and editing software often only accept MP3. The fix is a quick conversion — done privately.

## Why convert M4A to MP3

- **Broad compatibility.** MP3 plays almost everywhere.
- **Easy sharing.** People expect MP3 attachments.
- **Editing.** Many editors handle MP3 more smoothly than M4A.

## Convert locally

Our [M4A to MP3 tool](/tools/m4a-to-mp3) uses ffmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running in your browser:

1. Add your M4A file.
2. The audio is decoded and re-encoded on your device.
3. Download the MP3.

**Nothing is uploaded.** The recording is processed in your tab and the MP3 comes back to you. For voice memos that might contain personal or work talk, keeping it local is the point.
